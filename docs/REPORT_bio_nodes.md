## Schema notes

Same fields as `ee_*.json` and `body_*.json`: `id, title, kind, template, scale_m, area, parents, parts, made_of, processes, related, mesh, needs, summary, layers{basics,deeper,expert}, facts, formula, did_you_know, certainty, open_questions, refs, ext, kn, molecules, where, lab, steps`.
- `parents` = the section hub (`bio_*`) and every hub has parent root `biology_degree`; `parts` is the auto-filled inverse of `parents` among new nodes. Existing nodes were not edited, so reused nodes do not list new children in `parts`.
- `needs` = prerequisites (new nodes may need reused ones such as `limits_continuity`, `ode_first_order`, `random_variables`). `related` is used for soft links, including links to the 16 existing physiology processes (`cellular_respiration`, `protein_synthesis`, `mitosis`...), which are listed in the Processes section.
- `where` is one of the allowed place ids (`place:cell`, `place:dna`, `place:leaf`...), `mol:<id>` for molecules, or an existing body node id (`neuron`, `heart`, `retina`, `nephron`, `brain`...). Nodes with no sensible physical home (statistics, lab methods) have no `where`.
- Process nodes: template `process`, 6 steps each, `focus` ids validated against nodes, `mol:` ids or places.
- `translation_process` was **not** created: the existing `protein_synthesis` (human-body process) is reused, and `bio_translation_steps` and `bio_trna_translation_machinery` add the details.

## Open questions (C2-C4)

- `bio_protein_folding` (C2): Predicting folding pathways and kinetics, not just final structures, remains open.
- `bio_endosymbiosis` (C2): The exact nature of the host cell and the timing of mitochondrial acquisition relative to eukaryotic complexity (mitochondria-early vs late) remain debated.
- `bio_telomeres_senescence` (C2): How much replicative senescence and telomere erosion contribute to organismal ageing versus other hallmarks is unresolved.
- `bio_ecosystem_services` (C2): Monetary valuation of ecosystem services is methodologically contested and highly assumption-dependent.
- `bio_astrobiology` (C3): Whether life exists or ever existed beyond Earth is unknown.
- `bio_species_concepts` (C2): No single species concept works for all life; the boundaries are a matter of convention in many groups.
- `bio_neutral_theory_drift` (C2): The proportion of genomic divergence driven by positive selection versus drift is still disputed.
- `bio_human_evolution` (C2): Exact branching of early Homo lineages and the geography of H. sapiens origins (multiregional African model) are debated.
- `bio_origin_of_life` (C3): Whether life began as RNA, metabolism, lipids or something else is unresolved.
- `bio_rna_world_luca` (C3): Whether an RNA world preceded DNA/protein life is unproven.
- `bio_animal_features` (C2): Whether sponges or comb jellies are sister to all other animals is unsettled.
- `bio_chromatin_remodeling_histone_code` (C2): Which histone modifications cause, rather than follow, changes in transcription remains contested.
- `bio_evolution_of_flight` (C2): Whether bird flight arose from ground-up or arboreal origins is not settled.
- `bio_deuterostome_protostome` (C2): The phylogenetic position of Xenacoelomorpha affects interpretations of early bilaterian evolution.
- `bio_ecosystem_stability` (C2): How general and predictable ecological tipping points are remains uncertain.
- `bio_characteristics_life` (C2): No consensus definition of life exists; astrobiology needs one that works for non-Earth chemistry.
- `bio_viruses_structure` (C2): The origin of viruses (before cells, escaped genes, or reduced cells) remains unresolved and may differ between groups.
- `bio_emerging_viruses` (C2): The precise origin of SARS-CoV-2 has not been conclusively established.
- `bio_human_microbiome` (C2): Which microbiome–disease associations are causal (versus consequences) is still mostly unresolved.
- `bio_epigenetics` (C2): How much epigenetic information is inherited across generations in mammals is unresolved.
- `bio_animal_form_scaling` (C2): The mechanistic origin and universality of the 3/4 exponent are still debated.
- `bio_synaptic_plasticity_memory` (C2): How long-term memory is physically encoded and maintained across molecular turnover is unresolved.
- `bio_sleep_circadian` (C2): The core biological function of sleep is not settled.
- `bio_nervous_disorders` (C2): The causal role of amyloid and tau in Alzheimer's disease and best therapeutic targets are still debated.
- `bio_migration_navigation` (C2): The molecular basis of magnetoreception in animals is not established.

- Reused body nodes are placed in the animal physiology, neuroscience, immunology and processes sections; their own `parents` still point to the body systems, so the world view and the track show two different hierarchies for them.
- `where` assigns no place for proteins (1UBQ/1CRN/1BNA/4HHB/1EMA are only linked as `ext`); a `protein:<pdb>` where-type would let the 3D world host them. `molecules` ids not in `molecules_all.json` (e.g. plain `glucose`; use `glucose_alpha`/`glucose_beta`) were avoided.
