    Everything, at its true size.

    A proton, a cell, a park, the Earth, the Milky Way: one world you can zoom through. Here is all you need.

    1. Scroll to zoom.Scroll, or pinch on a phone, to move toward whatever is under the pointer. You can keep going in or out across more than 40 powers of ten. Drag to look around.
    2. Click a thing, then double-click it.One click or tap selects a thing: the view moves to frame it and the panel on the right says what it is, what it is made of and how it works (Basics, Deeper or Expert). A double click or double tap looks inside it: its cut-open view if it has one, otherwise the next level down at that spot.
    3. Watch the ruler.The scale down the left edge shows how big your view is. Click a place on it, such as Your skin, London or the Milky Way, to fly there. On a phone, tap the strip, or choose Library and open Places on the scale.
    Where am I?The path at the top of the panel (Universe, Milky Way, Earth and so on) shows where you are. Click a step to fly out to it, or press Back to return. One panelEverything to read is in the panel on the right: the place you are in, the thing you selected, a question trail, notes on what a picture is. Minimise folds it to a tab at the edge; it then stays folded until you click a thing or the tab. Find anythingUse the search field at the top right (heart, gold, caffeine, London, mitosis, a battery) to find places, elements, molecules, topics and 51 open textbooks. Library lists them to browse. Press / from anywhere to jump to the field. Speed meterAdd ?stats=1 to the address to see frames per second and what the drawing costs (Shift+P on a keyboard). On a phone the panel scrolls, and Hide closes it. Tours and tracksTour in or Tour out flies the whole way, one stop at a time. Tracks are guided paths through a subject.
